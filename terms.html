JARVIS Backup - Terms of Use

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Personal Use

JARVIS Backup is a private application intended exclusively for backup and recovery by its authorized owner. It is not offered as a public service.

Google Drive

When enabled, the application uses Google Drive to store and retrieve backup archives created through its authorized integration.

Security

The intended configuration encrypts backup contents locally before upload. Access credentials must remain private.

Availability

Backup and recovery operations depend on the availability of the local system and the connected storage provider.
